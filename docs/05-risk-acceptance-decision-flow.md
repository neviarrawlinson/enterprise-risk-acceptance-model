# Risk Acceptance Decision Flow

## Step 1: Risk Identified
A control gap, vulnerability, or policy deviation is identified.

## Step 2: Initial Assessment
- Determine scope
- Assess inherent and residual risk
- Identify business impact
- Confirm remediation feasibility

## Step 3: Determine Tier
Route to Tier 1, 2, or 3 based on impact and escalation triggers.

Automatic escalation occurs when:
- Significant customer data exposure risk
- Regulatory liability potential
- Enterprise-wide impact
- Multiple renewals without remediation
- Active exploitation context

## Step 4: Documentation
Complete the Risk Acceptance Form including:
- Owner
- Compensating controls
- Expiration date
- Remediation plan

## Step 5: Approval & Recording
- Route to appropriate approvers
- Record decision in centralized system of record

## Step 6: Expiration & Review
- Reassess prior to expiration
- Renew, remediate, or close
