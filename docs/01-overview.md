# Unified Enterprise Risk Acceptance Framework

## Purpose

This framework establishes a single, scalable, and practical risk acceptance process designed for complex enterprises with multiple business units, legal entities, and varying operational maturity levels.

It replaces fragmented “policy exception” processes with a unified, accountable, and time-bound risk acceptance model.

## Design Principles

- One process across the enterprise
- Lightweight and operationally realistic
- Tiered approvals aligned to impact
- Time-bound and review-driven
- Explicit business ownership
- Centralized system of record

## Why This Matters

Without a unified risk acceptance model, organizations experience:
- Delays in operational decisions
- Inconsistent approval thresholds
- Forgotten exceptions without expiration
- Audit findings due to poor documentation
- Unclear ownership of residual risk

This model ensures that risk acceptance is deliberate, accountable, and auditable.
