# Risk Acceptance Metrics & Reporting Model

A risk acceptance program must be measurable. Metrics ensure risk acceptance is not used as a permanent bypass mechanism and that ownership remains visible.

## Core Metrics

### 1. Open Risk Acceptances by Tier
- Count of active Tier 1 / Tier 2 / Tier 3
- Distribution by business unit / legal entity
- Trend over time

### 2. Decision Time by Tier (SLA Performance)
- Tier 1: target < 48 hours
- Tier 2: target < 10 business days
- Tier 3: target < 15 business days

### 3. Expirations & Hygiene
- Expiring in 30/60/90 days
- Past-due (expired but still open)
- Closure rate vs renewal rate

### 4. Renewal Frequency
- Renewed 2+ times
- Renewed with no remediation progress

### 5. Theme Tracking
- Volume by domain (Infra, AppSec, IAM, Vendor, Privacy)
- Top recurring control gaps

## Executive Dashboard (Quarterly View)
Keep it lightweight:
- Total accepted risks (by tier)
- Tier 3 summary (top 5)
- Upcoming expirations
- Themes and hotspots
- Remediation progress trend

## Program Success Indicators
- One unified acceptance process in use
- SLA targets consistently met
- 100% of acceptances have expiry dates
- Reduction in repeat renewals without progress
