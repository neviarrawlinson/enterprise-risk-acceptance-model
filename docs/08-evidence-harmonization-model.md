# Evidence Harmonization Model

Modern security programs suffer from redundant evidence collection across compliance frameworks. This model promotes a “collect once, reuse everywhere” approach.

---

## Core Principle

Evidence should be tied to controls, not frameworks.

Controls map to multiple regulatory obligations, but documentation should be centralized.

---

## Example Control Mapping

| Control Theme | CIS Control | PCI Requirement | NIS2 Alignment | CCPA Consideration |
|---------------|-------------|----------------|---------------|--------------------|
| Access Control | CIS 5 | PCI 7 | Identity & Access Mgmt | Data Protection |
| Vulnerability Management | CIS 7 | PCI 11 | Risk Management | Security Safeguards |
| Logging & Monitoring | CIS 8 | PCI 10 | Incident Detection | Audit Capability |

---

## Operational Model

1. Define master control set (CIS-aligned)
2. Map controls to regulatory obligations
3. Store evidence centrally
4. Reuse evidence across:
   - PCI audits
   - Privacy assessments
   - Internal risk reviews
   - External certifications

---

## Benefits

- Reduced audit fatigue
- Faster compliance cycles
- Clear control ownership
- Simplified reporting
- Improved executive visibility
