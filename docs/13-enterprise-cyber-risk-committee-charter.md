# Enterprise Cyber Risk Committee Charter

## 1. Purpose

The Enterprise Cyber Risk Committee (ECRC) provides structured oversight of material cyber risks and formal risk acceptance decisions.

The committee ensures that residual risk exposure aligns with organizational risk appetite and strategic objectives.

---

## 2. Scope

The ECRC reviews:

- Tier 3 (Material) risk acceptance requests
- Risks with regulatory or contractual implications
- Repeated risk renewals without remediation progress
- Enterprise-wide control deficiencies
- Significant cyber incidents requiring post-event governance review

---

## 3. Authority

The ECRC has authority to:

- Approve or reject material risk acceptance requests
- Require remediation plans
- Mandate compensating controls
- Escalate risks to executive leadership
- Adjust risk tolerance thresholds where appropriate

---

## 4. Membership

Core Members:
- Chief Information Security Officer (Chair)
- Cyber Security Risk Manager
- IT Leadership Representative
- Legal / Compliance Representative
- Privacy Officer (as applicable)

Ad Hoc Members:
- Business Unit Risk Owners
- Vendor Management Lead
- Incident Response Lead

---

## 5. Meeting Cadence

- Monthly standing meeting
- Ad hoc sessions for urgent escalations
- Quarterly executive summary review

---

## 6. Decision Framework

Each reviewed risk must include:

- Residual risk score
- Business impact narrative
- Compensating controls
- Remediation timeline
- Risk appetite alignment assessment

Decisions must be documented and stored in the centralized system of record.

---

## 7. Reporting

The ECRC provides:

- Quarterly risk exposure summary
- Tier 3 risk trend analysis
- Expiration and renewal reporting
- SLA performance metrics
- Emerging risk themes

Reports are distributed to executive leadership.

---

## 8. Accountability

Risk ownership remains with the business risk owner.

The committee provides oversight, not operational control.

No risk acceptance may remain open without review.

---

## 9. Review & Amendments

This charter shall be reviewed annually or following significant organizational or regulatory changes.
